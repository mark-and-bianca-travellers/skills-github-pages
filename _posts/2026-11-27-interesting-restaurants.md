There are so many interesting places to eat in novel locations.  Whilst in Chamarel, high in the mountains above the south-western coast, there are several restaurants with incredible views.  Perhaps the finest that we found is Le Chamarel, having the most incredible views of Le Morne and the surrounding coastline.  
  
![inside the Le Chamarel restaurant]({{"/assets/images/chamarel_inside.png"|relative_url}})  
  
![view of le Morne from Le Chamarel restaurant]({{"/assets/images/restaurant_view.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Along with the amazing views, the breakfast is lovely (and oddly inexpensive - MRU850 / £14 each).  
  
![le Chamarel breakfast]({{"/assets/images/chamarel_breakfast.png"|relative_url}})  
  
So nice, we breakfasted there on both Wednesday and Friday this week.  Surprisingly, to us at least, there were not too many people there (on either day). We meet so many interesting people in the majority of places we go, and Le Chamarel was no exception.  This morning we met Lynn and Phil, another two British retirees, like us, traveling to the most interesting parts of the World - to be honest, they have definitely been to more exotic places than we have.  We shared notes on places we’d visited, and Bianca explained where we had most enjoyed in Mauritius.  Now that we have returned to Pointe D’Esny (where we began our Mauritian journey) we hope to have breakfast again with them, tomorrow, at Naked Foods.  
  
![Lynn and Phil at Le Chamarel]({{"/assets/images/lynn_phil.png"|relative_url}})  
  
As a complete alternative to breakfast in the mountains, on Wednesday evening we took dinner aboard a floating restaurant in the Grande Rivière Noire Bay, north of le Morne, 40 or so minutes drive from our accommodation in Chamarel.  A small water taxi met us at the Le Morne Angler’s Club to ferry us to the restaurant boat. Stepping from the pier to the taxi, and from the taxi to the boat was quite challenging, particularly at the pier as the tide was not at its highest.  There was a good chance we might have ended up in the sea!  
  
![moon bark restaurant at sea]({{"/assets/images/restaurant_at_sea.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Again the food was amazing, and again we met a fascinating retired couple to talk with.  This time, Frank and Eileen from Oslo.  This all made for another memorable evening - and a drive back to our accommodation along the most snake-like road in the dark, with multiple sharp bends and complete hairpins.  We survived!
