We’ve been out on the local bus.  
  
![view from the bus]({{"/assets/images/bus_view.png"|relative_url}})  
  
With all the time in the world to prepare, we still didn’t remember some basics - shampoo and the like - plus we wanted to buy some fruit for our meal this evening.  Whilst we could eat out every night, neither of us wanted to return home the size of a horse!  
  
Buses seem to stop anywhere on their route; just raise a hand as one is going by and they’ll stop.  As our accommodation is on the route to Mahebourg, the buses go by every 30 minutes or so.  Fortunately, our destination was the last stop, so no worrying about where to get off!  
  
![the market]({{"/assets/images/market.png"|relative_url}})  
  
The market was incredible, with hundreds of stalls selling all-sorts of everything.  Everything seemed to be going on in French, but people were happy enough to speak with us in English.  Mauritius gained its independence from the UK in 1968, and the school system is basically still as it was, so most lessons are taught in English.  They drive on the left, too.  
  
After diving into a bar for a quick drink - a high of 27° today - we walked along the seafront before heading back to the market to make our purchases of fresh fruit and then to hunt for the correct bus to take us back.
