One of the lovely things about staying here in Bridget’s house is having Smita on hand to organise things for us - and generally tidy up, though we try to keep the house as orderly as possible.  As a treat, at a small cost, Smita will also cook a meal for you.  Travelling can cause havoc with eating sensibly, so we have been trying to eat just twice a day.  Often breakfast at Naked and dinner in the Bamboo Bar, or one of those replaced by a healthy fruit salad.  We planned for Smita to make a fish curry for our Thursday dinner, but forgot that she finished working at the house at 2pm.  Fish curry for Thursday lunch it was.    
  
![lunch prepared by Smita]({{"/assets/images/smita_lunch.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Just after 1pm Smita arrived at our terrace with bowls of the wonderful meal that she had prepared for us.  There was no way that we could eat it all, but we tried very hard.  It was delicious.  We were completely stuffed.  
  
Though we still managed a trip to Mahébourg on the bus for a beer, we found ourselves ready for bed well before 9pm!
