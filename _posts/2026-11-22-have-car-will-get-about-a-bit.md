 Our accommodation (Bridget’s place) is well situated on the coastal road, a brief bus ride to Mahébourg and a shortish walk to Blue Bay (via Bamboo Bar and Naked Foods, of course).  But to visit places broader afield, a car is necessary.  With Smita’s help (always on hand) we’ve hired a car. A neon green Jimny!  It was supposed to be a Swift, but the hire company is very busy, so we were upgraded.  Great!  Unlike our Bahamas experience, this car works, without the person knocking from inside the glove compartment each time it starts (see earlier blogs!). It has a higher ground clearance, which is going to be really useful, because some of the roads we are due to drive on are not paved and likely very bumpy.  
  
![vivid car]({{"/assets/images/vivid_car.png"|relative_url}})  
  
So, we’ve been out and about, seeing things and places in the south-east of the island that we may not once we move to our next accommodation in the mountains.  Mauritius is (was) volcanic, so some of the landscape is accordingly breathtaking. Pont Naturel is one such place, where an ancient lava-flow meets the Indian Ocean.  Here you can see where the action of the sea on the volcanic rock has left behind a ‘bridge’, several meters above the water.  We watched a family cross the bridge before we attempted it (haha), but actually it looked narrower than it actually was.  And the sea wasn’t rough enough to wash us off!  It did make for some great photos.
