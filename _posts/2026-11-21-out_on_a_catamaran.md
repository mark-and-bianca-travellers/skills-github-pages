A short distance from the house along the shoreline is a place where you can take a catamaran excursion.  Organised by Smita, so everyone was so pleasant to us, we booked a trip to take us out into the reef from 9am through to 4pm: a whole day of sailing, snorkelling, and sightseeing, drinking and eating!  
  
![everyone on the boat]({{"/assets/images/all_aboard.png"|relative_url}})  
  
A cab collected us at 08:45 to take us the short journey down the road where our catamaran was waiting. We arrived first, and waited for 30 minutes or so for the other 20 people that were sailing with us. The majority French, with a handful of Germans and a young Polish couple (who must have wondered what they were doing on a boat full of people over 55!  
  
As soon as we were aboard there was a constant supply of drink, biscuits and cake, as we set out into a beautiful blue sea under a blue sky and somewhere between 26° and 29°C.

![catamaran]({{"/assets/images/catamaran.png"|relative_url}})
  
After sailing for a couple of hours, enjoying the weather and the hospitality, we weighed anchor to allow those that wanted to swim and snorkel in the warm blue sea.  It was idyllic.  
  
![snorkelling]({{"/assets/images/snorkelling.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Once everyone was back on board, we ate and drank some more before setting off to the Grande Rivière, a river with an attractive waterfall and many mischievous monkeys!  The river was a little too narrow (or the cat too big) for the catamaran to sail up it sufficiently far to see the waterfall, so we all transferred to a couple of tenders.  A race ensued as to which tender would get back to the catamaran first.  I think it was a tie!  
  
![tender]({{"/assets/images/tender.png"|relative_url}})

![tender and catamaran]({{"/assets/images/tender_cat.png"|relative_url}})
  
Once back on the boat, lunch was served.  A barbecue of fish and chicken, prepared and served by the crew.  So much food!  Then we were off further north to a small island where we disembarked for an hour or so, where we could enjoy the sun on a gorgeous beach.  Bianca and I found a bar for a beer!  
  
![catamaran lunch]({{"/assets/images/cat_food.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Once back on the boat, we basked in the sun - trying not to fry - as we made our way leisurely back to where we began.  Our driver was waiting for us to take us back our accommodation.

![a sailer]({{"/assets/images/sailor.png"|relative_url}})
  
A wonderful day!
