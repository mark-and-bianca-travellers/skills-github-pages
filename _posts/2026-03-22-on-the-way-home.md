This morning was our last morning waking up to a Bahamian sunrise, for a little while at least.  The weather is as good as it has ever been today, so we take the opportunity to sit on the deck for a brief while before getting ourselves ready for the trip back to the UK.

![On the deck]({{"/assets/images/on_the_deck.png"|relative_url}})

  
The first part of our journey is the flight from Eleuthera to Nassau, a short 15 minute hop on a 42-seat aeroplane, that we have grown quite used to, flying between the Bahamian islands.  The flight is just after four in the afternoon, so we break the journey to the airport with lunch at “The Other Side”, a delightful restaurant (with the bumpiest approach road), literally on the other side of the bay from our accommodation.  Why we hadn’t found it earlier, I don’t know, but it was recommended to us by some local people (Lesley and Ken) we were talking with in the Glass Window Bar, last night.  It was absolutely amazing - a fantastic view of the bay and great food.  With four hours before our flight and 30 minutes to drive, we ate and drank slowly, enjoying the atmosphere.  We began to consider how we might stay on Eleuthera a little longer… unfortunately the flights are not exchangeable!

![View of New Moon Bay]({{"/assets/images/bay_view.png"|relative_url}})

  
We finally leave for the airport, return the hire car and join the flight for the beginning of our journey.  This has truly been the most fantastic first trip in our exploration of places far and wide.  It will be good to be home, but sad to leave such a laid-back, slow-paced and friendly place.
