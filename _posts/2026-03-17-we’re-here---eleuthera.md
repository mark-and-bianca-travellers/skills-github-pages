Yes, we’re here in Eleuthera.  In fact, this is the end of the first day, that began with a torrential downpour and ended with (another) cocktail at the Glass Window Bar, with blue skies and 25°C, again.  
  
![Tom’s place]({{"/assets/images/toms_place.png"|relative_url}})  
  
We are settled at Tom’s place in Whale Point, the quirkiest of places we have so far stayed.  A large, octagonal room with a pitched ceiling, the four-poster bed in the middle, with sofas, cooking facilities and washing facilities organised around the room.  Just one room 😬.  
  
![inside Tom’s]({{"/assets/images/octagonal.png"|relative_url}})  
  
This week in the Bahamas is going to be a little dodgy, weather-wise, giving us torrential rain this morning (and exposing a couple of leaks in the roof, though nothing to be concerned about), followed by wonderful sunshine for the rest of the day.  
  
After sitting out on the balcony for a while, we set off to explore the South of the island, eventually arriving at Governor’s Harbour, by way of some other lovely beaches; Rainbow Beach in particular.  
  
![Rainbow Beach]({{"/assets/images/rainbow_beach.png"|relative_url}})   
  
At the quay we found a little shop cum café, but it was closing.  The power was off to this part of the island because of the earlier storm.  Worry not.  The proprietor of the café suggested another place and then rang ahead to let them know we were on our way.  Five minutes later, we were at the Blue Room.  Even without electricity, they cooked up fried chicken and chips, served to us on a table of blue and white checks, *without *aircon.  It was lovely.  

![sign for the Blue Room Restaurant ]({{"/assets/images/blue_room_sign.png"|relative_url}})
  
With the day drawing to a close - driving in the dark is not recommended - we made our way back to Whale Point, stopping only at a convenience store for urgent supplies.  Before our turn off the main highway is the Glass Mirror Bar and, as we’d had such a good time there Monday night, we had to visit again.  Christine was ready to serve us and listen to our days adventures.  She’s going to Spanish Wells tomorrow, and so are we, so we plan to meet-up for a coffee and more stories of the island.
