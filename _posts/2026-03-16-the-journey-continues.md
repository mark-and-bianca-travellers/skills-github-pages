It’s the morning after the night of the beers, as we continue our journey to Eleuthera.  Timings have been a little “sub-optimal”, so we’re now in the airport terminal at midday, waiting for our 3pm flight!

![A rainbow]({{"/assets/images/rainbow.png"|relative_url}})
  
As usual, we were awake at 5-ish and looking on Maps for a place for breakfast.  We found somewhere called Louis & Steen’s, only thirty or so minutes away, walking slowly.  We got ready and set out.  We hadn’t got far when it began to spit with rain.  Just a little, but enough to have us check the forecast to see 0.00mm was expected.  We carried on.  As we figured out the safest way to walk, we really had arrived at the point of no return.  The heavens had truly opened, but carrying on didn’t seem to be any worse an option than continuing to the restaurant.   
  
![Bianca walking in the rain ]({{"/assets/images/rain_waking.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Within a few minutes, the sky was dark and the rain was falling in sheets. We ducked into the scrubland beside the road and stood under a palm tree whilst the worst of the rain passed over.  It wasn’t the best of cover: we were both soaked!  
  
![A very wet Mark and Bianca ]({{"/assets/images/wet_us.png"|relative_url}})  
  
Dodging puddles and splashes from cars, we arrived at L&S.  After ordering, we dashed into the restroom to dry ourselves (well, our hair at least) with paper towels.  
  
Breakfast was lovely, and the weather had returned to the usual sunshine by the time we left the restaurant; our clothes had dried just a little, too.  Walking back we received a call from the owner of the accommodation we had booked in Eleuthera (and paid for, through Expedia) to let us know that the property wasn’t available - there had been some sort of a mix up between him and the agent! 🤬. As we didn’t want to have a conversation along the roadside, I said we’d call back once we were back at our apartment.  In the intervening twenty minutes we spoke with Thomas two or three times more, as he called with variations on a solution until, by the time we were in a position to talk properly, the apparent double-booking was resolved.  Hopefully, all will be good by the time we arrive there this afternoon.  
  
Meanwhile, it’s probably time to find another place to sit in this airport before we (eventually) board our flight 😃.
